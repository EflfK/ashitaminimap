visible structure asset is authored.

- Detour source: `Al_Zahbi.nav` from xiNavmeshes commit
  `d5de48de84868bde744e4864768a611e5aad82b0`, SHA-256
  `2C18F83E8FA238C4EADFBB4BB402DB4621012D0D4F933FB41481A789C1E494CC`.

  `assets/world/connections.lua`, covering Whitegate, Bhaflau Thickets, and

- Home Points and Survival Guides: verified empty for this zone.
- Fixed Treasure Chests and Treasure Coffers: verified empty.
- Fixed supported NM references: verified empty. Besieged/event actors are not

## Component selection

The six exact handoff endpoints select the 498-node public city network. The
complete source contains 1,109 nodes; the remaining disconnected geometry is
not attached to a supported destination or physical world handoff and is not
promoted into the production graph. No inferred connection, transition,
blocked link, mask, page rule, or elevation band is used.

# Valkurm Dunes (zone 103)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes native Detour topology
selected by every exact world handoff, the Survival Guide, and supported
fixed public NM references. It requires no structure image and authors no
transition or blocked-link exception.

The production graph preserves two disconnected public components:

- the 7,355-node main Dunes surface contains Selbina, La Theine, Konschtat,
  the Survival Guide, and Valkurm Emperor; and
- the 545-node western/Gusgen component contains the Gusgen handoff, Golden
  Bat, Marchelute, Doman, and Onryo.

One nearby-component lead may represent the missing public connector between
these areas, but proximity is not proof. It remains disconnected until an
attended entrance/midpoint/exit capture verifies a physical route.

## Stock calibration

The imported stock catalog identifies one page, `m_103_00`. Runtime stock
calibration should remain authoritative through `stock_calibration = true`.
The installed `FFXiMain.dll` did not expose the expected static table
signature and AshitaMCP was unavailable during this audit, so the exact scale
byte and signed record offsets remain unresolved. This blocks a completed-map
claim but does not change graph world coordinates.

## Native-component review

The unseeded native graph has 19,036 nodes, 19,711 edges, and 1,210
components. The production selector retains 7,900 nodes, 9,120 edges, and
two components:

| Nodes | Evidence and disposition |
|---:|---|
| 7,355 | Main Dunes surface; Selbina, La Theine, Konschtat, Survival Guide, and Valkurm Emperor |
| 545 | Western/Gusgen area; Gusgen handoff, Golden Bat, Marchelute, Doman, and Onryo |

The other 1,208 components contain no exact handoff or supported public fixed
destination. They are mostly isolated collision fragments, decorative or
inaccessible surfaces, and water/event geometry and are not promoted by
two-dimensional proximity.

The selected graph has no stacked-floor overlap and one substantial
nearby-component lead:

```text
main Dunes:    (-579.000,251.375,20.300)
Gusgen area:   (-579.333,244.083,17.867)
planar gap:    7.300 yalms
elevation gap: 2.433 yalms
```

The elevation values are Detour elevation, not live player Z. No transition is
authored without live traversal evidence.

and 4.725 yalms for Enkelados, 1.923 for Mimas, 3.159 for Porphyrion, 9.726 for
Pallas, 6.830 and 5.900 for Ixtab, 6.861 for Alkyoneus, and 3.255 for Autarch.

Mimas, 60.612 from Mimas to Porphyrion, 110.163 from Pallas to eleventh-floor
Ixtab, 115.403 from twelfth-floor Ixtab to Alkyoneus, and 69.819 from Alkyoneus
to Autarch. The two Enkelados points route to one another in 342.505 yalms.

The path validator reports 1,761 nodes and 1,914 connections with no one-way
edges. The bounded native-edge audit reports exact parity, no inferred-edge
delta, and no candidate cross-component joins. Two fresh three-seed
regenerations are byte-identical to production at SHA-256
`1E9BD0E2E06DC5A8F7AC6DC17C36D45B92303536384CC527FB16A040CACEFEC4`, so no
graph change was needed. Existing authored elevator handoffs remain
transitions without false projected links.

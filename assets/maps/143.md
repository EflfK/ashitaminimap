# Palborough Mines (zone 143)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes native Detour topology
for the active Home Point, both sides of every exact world handoff, all twelve
active Treasure Chest possibilities, and the supported fixed-NM spawn
references. It requires no structure image and authors no transition or
blocked-link exception.

Palborough Mines has three stock map pages and multiple overlapping floors.
The pinned NPC records prove two elevator mechanisms and a teleporter pair,
but they do not provide attended entrance, midpoint, and exit route samples.
No elevator, teleporter, or cross-floor connection is inferred from projected
X/Y proximity.

## Stock calibration

The imported stock catalog has pages `1`, `2`, and `3`, with page `1` as the
default. Runtime `stock_calibration = true` is authoritative for the exact
FFXiMain map-table scale and origin.

This session did not have pinned static captures of the numeric calibration
records or evidence assigning retained navigation components to individual
stock pages. The graph therefore remains page-independent and partial rather
than guessing page ownership.

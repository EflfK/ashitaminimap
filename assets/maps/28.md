# Sacrarium (zone 28)

## Status and pinned sources

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes the single substantial
native Detour network selected by exact physical-boundary, arrival, travel,
treasure, moving-quest, named-encounter, and ordinary-mob evidence. It does
not invent door-state links, trap behavior, event transport, mission access,
or joins to collision fragments.

## Stateful doors and mechanisms

The zone script changes eighteen labyrinth doors every Vana'diel day according
to an eight-day open/closed table. The Iron Gate, seven principal Wooden
Gates, additional wooden gates, Reliquiarium Gate, switches, large and small
keyholes, Papal Chambers door, and mission/cutscene actors are also stateful.
The native mesh spans the ordinary room network, but no current-day door
state, transition, blocked link, trap, keyhole effect, or mission bypass is
authored. Routes crossing a closed door remain an attended limitation.

The remote mission question mark, Diabolos chamber actors, Papal Chambers
actors, stale-draft props as travel points, replay NPCs, moogles, achievement
and Unity helpers, zero-coordinate records, and custom level-84/86 Aqueduct
Spiders are excluded from the static catalog.

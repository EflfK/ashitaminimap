# Lower Delkfutt's Tower (zone 184)

## Routing and NM-layer completion

The exact runtime `FFXiMain.dll` stock calibration for pages `1`, `2`, `3`,
and `15` remains authoritative. The shared 3D public graph retains the main
tower and Eurymedon's native room component; no structure image is required.

Minimap.dll can retain floor-1 page `1` after both in-zone climbs and after the
post-elevator descent enters the basement. Live player state at
`(531.770, 20.000, 16.000)` confirmed that the basement incorrectly retained
page `1`; the nearby live Cermet Door at `(520.437, 20.025, 16.000)` confirmed
the active level. Earlier states at `(583.4, 31.1, -16.0)` and
`(526.5, -46.2, -30.2)` reproduced the same stale fallback on floors 2 and 3.
The levels occupy distinct 16-yalm elevation bands in the retained navmesh.
Automatic `page_rules` now select basement page `15` at live Z `8` or above,
page `2` from Z `-24..-8`, and page `3` at or below `-24`, using the midpoints
between the floor plateaus as handoffs. Floor 1 continues to use normal
stock-page selection.

## Active notorious monsters

Levels, spawn behavior, placeholder relationships, and coordinates come from
the pinned CatsEyeXI `mob_groups.sql`, `mob_spawn_points.sql`, `IDs.lua`, and
individual zone mob scripts. Script tuples use `(x, live-z, map-y)` and are
converted into the catalog's `(x, y, z)` fields.

| NM | Level | Page / floor | Type | Placeholders | Scripted points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Epialtes | 28-32 | page 1 / first | Lottery | 2 | 1 |
| Tyrant | 40-42 | page 1 / first | Timed | 0 | 1 |
| Hippolytos | 28-32 | page 2 / second | Lottery | 2 | 1 |
| Eurymedon | 30-34 | page 3 / third | Lottery | 1 | 1 |

These page assignments are deterministic from the existing floor elevation
bands: Epialtes and Tyrant are around live Z `0`, Hippolytos is at `-16.099`,
and Eurymedon is at `-32`. There is no supported NM in the page-15 basement.
Each lottery script contains one NM spawn point; the placeholder counts reflect
the complete `phList`, not the number of marker possibilities.

Epialtes, Tyrant, and Hippolytos snap within 9.767 yalms in the main component.
Representative routes pass from the entrance to Epialtes (143.473 yalms),
Epialtes to Tyrant (400.904 yalms), and Tyrant to Hippolytos (565.289 yalms).

Eurymedon's third-floor room belongs to a native 24-node component omitted by
the former single-seed graph. Its verified spawn point is now an additional
component seed and snaps within 2.644 yalms. A route from the NM point to its
scripted Giant Sentry placeholder is 10.169 yalms. The component remains
separate because the source navmesh provides no connection to the main tower;
no transition or inferred edge was invented.

## Excluded records

- Disaster Idol is a Promathia 5-3 Tenzen's Path mission encounter spawned by
  checking its Cermet Door. Its pinned record is event-controlled and its mob
  script explicitly notes incomplete level-scaling behavior, so it is not
  presented as an ordinary huntable NM.
- Orna, the Fomorian Spears, and Illusory Pot are event/dynamic records without
  a supportable persistent NM lifecycle in the pinned zone scripts.
- Akvan is a Voidwatch/event record and is excluded from the ordinary NM layer.
- Gwyllgi, the Vanguard records, and Hydra Bard have no active zone spawn point
  in the pinned data. They are dormant/unplaced records.
- `Gigass Bat` and `Gigass Bats` are pet records used by Gigas NMs, not separate
  huntable notorious monsters.

All active, independently huntable named NMs with supportable positions are
therefore represented without displaying mission actors, pets, or dormant
database rows as regular map targets.

# Jugner Forest [S] (zone 82)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes eight native Detour
components selected by all exact physical thresholds and two additional exact
door-side regions. Campaign controls, Wooden Gates, Cavernous Maw travel,
Telepoint interaction, drops, and event relocations remain unlinked unless
their traversable before/after endpoints are explicitly supported.

The stock catalog contains only page `0`, which is also the default. The graph
remains zone-owned (`page_id = nil`) and should use live stock calibration via
`stock_calibration = true`.

## Native component selection

The unseeded native graph has 28,500 nodes, 30,547 bidirectional edges, and
1,294 components, including 665 isolated nodes. Eight exact supported
selectors, chosen in evidence-source order when they add a useful region,
retain 17,704 nodes, 20,156 edges, and these component sizes:

```text
10498, 3737, 3292, 133, 15, 12, 9, 8
```

The other 1,286 source components have no supported routing selector. The
Stone Monument selects an isolated node; Glowing Pebbles, `qm2`, `qm5`, and
Felled Trees select fragments of two to eight nodes without active scripts or
useful route extent. They are omitted pending attended/source classification.
Campaign/event actors with mismatched or zero positions do not select graph
components.

## Physical handoffs and controlled destinations

Seven distinct Jugner-side physical handoffs are supported: one with Batallia
Downs [S], three with Vunkerl Inlet [S], one with Batallia Downs, and two with
La Vaule [S]. Paired samples exist for the first five:


The zone default arrival equals the Vunkerl-north sample
`(621.865,300.264,-6.665)`. Physical records select components only; they do
not prove climb/drop directionality or cross-zone graph edges.

Six exact door records (`_2a0`, `_2a1`, `_2a2`, `_2a4`, `_2a5`, `_2a6`) and
the `_2a3` Wooden Gate remain destinations. `_2a5` exposes an event-controlled
outbound move to La Vaule [S], but that does not establish an ordinary walking
edge or the reverse arrival. The Cavernous Maw delegates to global event
logic, and the Telepoint grants a gate crystal rather than relocating the
player. No shortcut is authored for any of them.

Campaign arbiters, coffers, sentries, provisioners, fortifications, campaign
mobs, and temporary actors are not treated as routing transitions.

|---|---:|---|---:|

records, and event actors are excluded from the static catalog.

## Validation

- Native generation twice and inferred generation are byte-identical.
- Graph SHA-256:
  `C655A0960C04153384A413CFB056A035D4AF54188C2F6F30C343D3C41414C00A`
- `tools/validate_path_graphs.py assets/paths/82.lua` passes.
- Fifty-three exact boundary, arrival, door, travel, logging, treasure, quest,
  and NM samples snap within 0.483-22.079 yalms. Boundary samples use exact
  planar snapping because several paired records differ from navmesh-centroid
  elevation by more than four yalms.
- Twelve positive representative same-component routes pass, ranging from
  3.875 to 675.602 graph yalms.
- The graph has no isolated node, steep edge, or separated-floor overlap. It
  preserves three native edges longer than 24 yalms and six nearby-component
  review leads. No lead is promoted to a campaign, door, transport, stair, or
  drop transition.
- No mask, page override, transition, one-way transition, or blocked link was
  authored.

Attended completion requires page-0 calibration; classification of 1,286
unselected components, three long edges, and six nearby leads; live
before/after checks for all seven boundaries, every gate/door, Cavernous Maw,
Telepoint, campaign controls, and possible drops; and verification of the
Survival Guide, logging, coffers, Stone Monument, fragment destinations,
published and excluded NMs, long routes, and off-route recovery.

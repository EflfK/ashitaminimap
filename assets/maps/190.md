# King Ranperre's Tomb (zone 190)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes native Detour topology
for every exact physical handoff, all 14 active Treasure Chest possibilities,
and pinned fixed NMs without requiring or generating a structure image. No
transition or blocked-link exception is authored.

The selected graph has two large disconnected public components: the northern
entrance/chest network and the southern Cemetery Cherry/Vrtra network. Their
closest fragments overlap in X/Y at different elevations. They remain
disconnected pending attended entrance, midpoint, and exit evidence for the
real floor connector.

The imported stock catalog contains pages `1`, `2`, and `14`, with page `1`
the fallback default. Exact numeric map-table records and deterministic
page/elevation ownership were not captured in this session. The graph is
therefore page-independent and runtime stock calibration remains authoritative
through `stock_calibration = true`; exact page filtering remains unresolved.

The active Survival Guide at `(-452.571,-217.970,-19.807)` lies 186.425 yalms
from the nearest navmesh centroid. It is published as a marker but cannot
receive a route from this source.

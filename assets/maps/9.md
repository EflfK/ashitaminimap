# Pso'Xja (zone 9)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It retains every native component
selected by the exact zone boundary, in-zone teleport, active Home Point,
fixed chest, and supported NM evidence. It does not invent links across
teleports, timed elevators, trap doors, mission gates, page boundaries, or
overlapping floors.

The imported stock catalog contains pages `1` through `16`, with page `1` as
the default. The graph is deliberately zone-owned (`page_id = nil`) because
the pinned records do not assign the 18 retained components to stock pages.
No `page_graph_seeds`, page rules, or page transitions are published without
attended stock-page evidence.

## Graph audit

1,968 components. Exact handoff, travel, and supported-NM selection retains
2,668 nodes, 2,905 bidirectional edges, and thirteen components:


The isolated nodes and small fragments are retained only because exact
supported destinations select them. Production has no long or steep edge, no
projected separated-elevation overlap, and no substantial nearby-component
lead. The full graph has 1,047 isolated nodes, 103 long edges, 25 floor
overlaps, and 131 nearby-component leads; retaining it wholesale would be
unsafe. Of special interest is an excluded 6,072-node component near the
17-node Survival Guide component: their closest full-source nodes are only
1.900 yalms apart in plan but 9.333 yalms apart vertically. This is an
attended dock/floor lead, not proof of a link.

# Pashhow Marshlands [S] (zone 90)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes two independently
supported native components. Campaign controls, gates, doors, the Cavernous
Maw, Telepoint, transports, drops, and event relocations remain unlinked unless
their traversable before/after endpoints are explicitly supported.

The stock catalog contains only page `0`, which is the default. The graph is
zone-owned (`page_id = nil`) and should use `stock_calibration = true`.

## Native component selection

The unseeded native graph has 19,112 nodes, 21,093 bidirectional edges, and 403
components, including 218 isolated nodes. The first exact physical handoff and
the later Stone Monument selector retain two useful components of 4,844 and
3,797 nodes: 8,641 nodes and 9,932 edges total. The other 401 source components
have no supported routing selector. `qm3` selects a 13-node fragment without
supported behavior or useful route extent and is omitted.

## Physical handoffs and controlled destinations

Four paired physical boundaries are represented by eight exact samples: two
with Grauberg [S], one with Rolanberry Fields [S], and one with Beaucedine
Glacier [S]. All select the main component. The zone default is the exact
Rolanberry-side arrival `(547.841,696.323,23.192)`. These are component
selectors and destinations, not cross-zone graph edges.

Exact records for `_2i0`, Corroded Gate `_2i1`, `_2i2`, `_2i3`, `_2i4`, the
Corroded Door, Telepoint, and Cavernous Maw remain destinations only. No record
proves an ordinary walking edge, directionality, or relocation endpoint.
Campaign arbiters, coffers, sentries, suppliers, fortifications, forces,
transporters, Voidwatch actors, pets, helpers, and temporary actors are not
routing transitions.

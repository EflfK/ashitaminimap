substantial-component advisories. Production has 3,433 nodes, 3,859 edges,
and three components of 3,361, 68, and 4 nodes. It has no isolated node, steep
edge, overlap pair, or nearby substantial-component advisory; its 48 long

and excluded, including many substantial networks. This is intentionally not

selected public graph is independent of structure images.

- SHA-256: `3FE71D9249B3A34384BB3CC1651F484E2AB8F386B29639CEDBCDAEABC1841875`.
- Seeds: Survival Guide plus the north and southwest Outer Horutoto thresholds.
- Authored transitions: two localized Outer Horutoto entrance seams.

  SHA-256 `1AB0830EC93AA6E15C7E9714B46EAAB4D8457D53ED423373249377A462CEFA83`.

## Deterministic CatsEyeXI source

CatsEyeXI commit `314deaf03465f2b24b6a1e4e73a016ca036f1084` is the pinned
authority for this audit. The catalog was checked exhaustively against zone
`115` records in `sql/mob_groups.sql` and `sql/mob_spawn_points.sql`, plus
`scripts/zones/West_Sarutabaruta/IDs.lua`, `Zone.lua`, and the zone mob

its `z` field.

The same source supplies Survival Guide unlock bit `28`; there is no fixed
standard treasure set.

The complete active, supportable static catalog is:

| Name | Level | Type | Exact scripted starts | Placeholder count |

| Nunyenunc | 12 | Lottery/random start | 50 | 3 Carrion Crows |
| Numbing Norman | 12-13 | Timed/random start | 4 | 0 |

Tom Tit Tat's 50 possibilities are copied verbatim from
`mobs/Tom_Tit_Tat.lua`; its `phList` identifies the two immediately preceding
Mandragoras. Nunyenunc's 50 possibilities likewise come from
`mobs/Nunyenunc.lua`, whose `phList` identifies three immediately preceding
Carrion Crows. Numbing Norman's four possibilities come from
`mobs/Numbing_Norman.lua`; the script chooses a new point and applies a
20-minute respawn on initialization and despawn. The SQL initial coordinates
for these randomized actors are not substituted for their complete scripted
possibility arrays.

The following records are deliberately not promoted as static NM ranges:

- Yilbegan, Orcus, Farruca Fly, Jyeshtha, Rummager Beetle, and Raker Bee are
  state-driven Voidwalkers. Their `1,1,1` SQL placeholders are not spawn
  destinations; `IDs.lua` assigns them by abyssite tier and
  `scripts/globals/voidwalkerpos.lua` owns their changing positions.
- Virvatuli has three nonzero SQL actor records at `(120,-440,3.5)`,
  `(-441,-357,3.5)`, and `(0,560,-28.5)`, but these are Voidwatch/event
  positions rather than ordinary initial-spawn NM behavior.
- Bakru, Pyracmon, armor actors, herd/event actors, Astral Box, Spriggans, and
  The Doll Whisperer actors are zero-position, event-only, ordinary-regime, or
  inactive placeholder records. Garrison mobs and fished groups are likewise
  outside the static NM layer.

## Routing and validation

All 104 published NM possibilities were checked against the existing
production graph using the runtime's 24-yalm planar snap radius and four-yalm
floor tolerance. Every point snaps:

| Range | Minimum snap | Maximum snap | Failures |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tom Tit Tat (50) | 0.490 | 16.365 | 0 |
| Nunyenunc (50) | 0.831 | 9.100 | 0 |
| Numbing Norman (4) | 2.721 | 9.147 | 0 |

Because all points land on the existing single connected component, no NM
seed, `GraphJob` change, regenerated graph, or speculative transition is
required. The NM layer remains reference-only and performs no live tracking.

Validation for this catalog change includes an exact scripted-array/catalog
comparison, the focused path-graph validator, the zone-115 catalog audit, and
`git diff --check`.

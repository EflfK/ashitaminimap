dimensional portals, and two Swirling Vortexes have exact NPC positions, but
they are not ordinary graph edges. One updraft sends the player to Sealion's
Den; five updrafts relocate internally to `(-25.000,-620.000,-1.000)`. The
dimensional portals event-transport to La Theine Plateau, Konschtat Highlands,
and Tahrongi Canyon. The vortex scripts are TODO/commented. Rubious Crystal
endpoint pairing is not proved by the pinned sources. No transition,
directionality, or permanence is inferred from these mechanisms.

Home Points, boundaries, and all non-pet ordinary mob rows were checked as
exact component evidence. Every ordinary destination resolves to one of the
four retained surfaces. Zero-coordinate helpers, mission/cutscene actors,
moogles, replay helpers, pets, encounter adds, and mechanism-only collision
fragments are excluded.

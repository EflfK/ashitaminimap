# Outer Horutoto Ruins (zone 194)

## Routing and NM-layer completion

The exact runtime stock calibration for pages `1`, `2`, `3`, `4`, `5`, and
`14` remains authoritative. One shared 3D graph intentionally preserves four
entrance-separated ruins; no structure image is required.

The graph and the CatsEyeXI mob records do not provide a deterministic mapping
from world position to a particular stock map page. NM markers therefore remain
unpaged and use `floor = 'UNASSIGNED'`; the runtime-selected stock page remains
authoritative. Assigning a page from visual resemblance would risk showing a
marker on the wrong overlapping ruins section.

- Graph: 1,677 nodes, 1,772 bidirectional edges, 4 components
  (`711`, `323`, `360`, and `283` nodes).
- SHA-256: `552F1D52A4B9D442244AF9946054A01985C5A570D8AD5822EB9DF9B4B0C17422`.
- Seeds: all four Sarutabaruta thresholds and the active chest.
- Nav source: xiNavmeshes `d5de48de84868bde744e4864768a611e5aad82b0`;
  SHA-256 `62427499FD292FDA59760D379FC0E4EE4ACD4250051A86438FADDAEEDF456CC4`.

CatsEyeXI has no Home Point or Survival Guide and supplies one active Treasure
Chest. All four exits snap correctly and long routes pass inside every
component. The components remain separate because the ruins are entered from
different outdoor thresholds. Two generations and native/inferred adjacency
are byte-identical.

## Active notorious monsters

NM levels, spawn types, placeholder relationships, and scripted spawn points
come from pinned CatsEyeXI commit
`314deaf03465f2b24b6a1e4e73a016ca036f1084`, using the zone's `mob_groups.sql`,
`mob_spawn_points.sql`, `IDs.lua`, `globals.lua`, and individual mob scripts.
The script coordinate order is converted from `(x, live-z, map-y)` into the
catalog's `(x, y, z)` fields.

The Balloon script shares two qualifying placeholders, one cooldown, and one
random selection across Bomb King and the two Doppelgangers. Each trio member
can use the same three scripted spawn points, so all three possibilities are
published for each NM rather than treating the database row as its only point.

All 13 distinct NM destinations snap to the retained graph within 5.380 yalms.
Representative connected routes pass from Desmodont to Legalox Heftyhind
(134.643 yalms), Desmodont to Ah Puch (531.249 yalms), across Ah Puch's range
(106.223 yalms), and from the western trio point to its other two possibilities
(317.708 and 362.537 yalms). The existing four-component graph already covers
every supported NM, so no graph seed, transition, or inferred edge was added.

## Excluded records

- Jack of Cups, Jack of Batons, Jack of Swords, Jack of Coins, Queen of Swords,
  and Queen of Coins are mission/event-controlled Cardian encounters, not
  persistent huntable NM spawns.
- `Custom Cardian` is a level-1 dynamic event record rather than a named,
  independently huntable NM.
- Voidwrought is a Voidwatch/event spawn with level `0` placeholder data in the
  pinned group table; it is not an ordinary active map-layer NM.
- No additional dormant NM row or script with a supportable active spawn was
  found in the pinned zone data.

These exclusions keep event actors and incomplete dynamic records from
appearing as ordinary always-relevant NM markers.

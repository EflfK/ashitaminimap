by the exact Buburimu handoff, Survival Guide, active chest positions, or
supported NMs are retained. No cave seam, ledge, drop, or proximity gap is
bridged.

Completion requires live stock calibration/page confirmation, attended
traversal captures for all route-affecting seams and floor changes,
classification of excluded components, and an addon reload with route and
recovery tests. Cross-component destinations remain marker-only.

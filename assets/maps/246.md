# Port Jeuno map provenance

- Zone: Port Jeuno (`246`)
- Installed vanilla DAT: `<FFXI>/ROM/18/78.DAT` (`m_246_00`)
- Collision/navigation: `Port_Jeuno.obj` and `Port_Jeuno.nav`
- Calibration: origin and H-8 grid center `(255, 256)`, `0.80 px/yalm`,
  `40 yalms/cell`
- View bounds: `(0,0)-(512,512)`

The installed DAT identifies its texture as DXT3 and does not use the paletted
layout handled by `generate_vanilla_map.py`. A guessed or third-party image is
not substituted, so Port Jeuno intentionally ships without the optional
vanilla layer. Its deterministic walkable structure is complete.

Port Jeuno's center street, western transition, and four airship arms are
separate navmesh components. They are selected with these nav-polygon interior
points (world X,Y):

```text
(-1.281, 2.167)       (-148.948, -1.833)
(-77.948, 119.500)    (-4.031, 119.333)
(-79.198, -117.000)   (-6.031, -119.333)
```

Disconnected collision islands and non-playable surfaces are omitted.

```text
python tools/generate_walkable_map.py <OBJ> <NAV> \
  assets/maps/246_structure.png --origin-x 255 --origin-y 256 \
  --pixels-per-yalm 0.8 \
  --seed=-1.281,2.167 --seed=-148.948,-1.833 \
  --seed=-77.948,119.500 --seed=-4.031,119.333 \
  --seed=-79.198,-117.000 --seed=-6.031,-119.333
```

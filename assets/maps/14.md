# Hall of Transference (zone 14)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It retains the three native chamber
components, the three Promyvion gate platforms, and the three outbound trigger
pads selected by pinned CatsEyeXI records. It does not invent walkable links
across event teleports, Promyvion mission gates, sky shortcuts, or the native
vertical seams between each gate platform and chamber.

The imported stock catalog has pages `1`, `2`, and `3`, with page `1` as the
default. The graph is deliberately zone-owned (`page_id = nil`) because the
pinned records do not assign the nine retained components to stock pages. No
page rule or page transition is published without attended page evidence.

## Native component selection

The unseeded native graph has 384 nodes, 366 bidirectional edges, and 43
components. It includes 23 isolated nodes. Nine deterministic source-order
selectors retain 334 nodes, 349 edges, and these component sizes:

```text
100, 95, 81, 16, 16, 14, 4, 4, 4
```

The first three selectors are the exact Holla, Dem, and Mea Cermet Gate NPC
positions. The next three are the arithmetic midpoints of the exact Holla,
Mea, and Dem outbound trigger cuboids; endpoints of those cuboids can snap to
an adjacent chamber instead, while the midpoint truthfully selects the active
trigger pad. The last three are exact first corners of the Mea, Holla, and Dem
sky-trigger cuboids and select the chamber components. This ordering follows
the pinned NPC and trigger-area source records.

The production audit has no isolated node, no steep edge, and no
separated-elevation overlap pair. It has one 28.375-yalm native authored edge,
nodes 116 to 121 at elevation `86.8`; native and inferred topology both retain
it. Three nearby substantial-component leads remain between the gate platforms
and chambers:

```text
Dem    planar 4.275, elevation 5.200, component sizes 14 / 100
Holla  planar 4.624, elevation 5.200, component sizes 16 / 95
Mea    planar 6.039, elevation 5.333, component sizes 16 / 81
```

The source scripts prove event-driven gates rather than ordinary walkable
seams, so none is authored as a transition. The other 34 native components
have no supported exact selector and remain excluded.

## Chambers, gates, and apparatus

The active Cermet Gates are:

```text
Holla  (-216.449, 280.000, -8.635)  -> Promyvion-Holla (zone 16)
Dem    (-216.449,-280.000,-48.635)  -> Promyvion-Dem   (zone 18)
Mea    ( 280.000, -16.450,-88.659)  -> Promyvion-Mea   (zone 20)
```

Their scripts require the relevant Chains of Promathia mission progress and
start zoning events. They snap within 0.431-0.568 yalms. There is no ordinary
route from a gate platform to its chamber, and no transition is published.

Each chamber contains two Large Apparatus records. The left apparatus records
have no response; the right records at `(-236.300,270.000,-4.900)`,
`(-236.300,-290.000,-44.900)`, and `(290.000,-36.299,-84.900)` accept the
corresponding Clear Chip when the sky-shortcut quest state allows it. The
actual sky transport is separately triggered by the chamber cuboid and may
break the registration chip. All six apparatus points snap within
2.772-3.494 yalms. Left-to-right chamber routes are 45.406, 43.834, and
54.127 yalms for Holla, Dem, and Mea respectively.

The default arrival `(274,-62,-82)` is retained and snaps at 3.174 yalms. It
routes 40.237 yalms to the Mea right apparatus.

## Outbound trigger areas

The exact trigger bounds prove three event exits to La Theine Plateau,
Tahrongi Canyon, and Konschtat Highlands. Their deterministic live-coordinate
midpoints are:

```text
Holla exit (-278.5, 280.5, -2.5)
Mea exit    ( 279.5, -78.5,-82.0)
Dem exit    (-279.5,-279.5,-42.5)
```

They snap within 0.548-1.795 yalms on three distinct four-node pads. The sky
trigger centers snap within 0.063-0.871 yalms on the chamber components. An
event trigger proves relocation, not an ordinary walkable connection, so no
cross-zone or in-zone shortcut edge is added. The world connection table has
no zone 14 record; the event scripts are the pinned evidence for these
transports.

## Travel, treasure, and NMs

There are no active Home Points or Survival Guides in the pinned records. The
active treasure table has no Hall of Transference entry, and there is no fixed
Treasure Chest or Treasure Coffer NPC. There is no zone 14 mob-group entry, so
there is no supported NM spawn range to publish. Cutscene actors, FXTEST,
Goblin Footprint, mission-only question marks, zero-coordinate NPCs, and
moogle/achievement helpers are excluded as non-routing destinations.

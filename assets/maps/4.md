# Bibiki Bay (zone 4)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes the two large native
Detour components selected by exact mainland and Purgonorgo destinations. It
does not connect the regions across the Manaclipper, retain time-dependent
boat surfaces, assign the graph to either stock page, or invent a link to any
of the 911 unselected source components.

The imported stock catalog contains pages `1` and `2`, with page `1` as the
default. The graph is deliberately zone-owned (`page_id = nil`) pending live
stock-page calibration and proof of page membership. Both retained regions
are far apart in world X/Y and remain disconnected in native topology.

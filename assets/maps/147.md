# Beadeaux map provenance

## Completion status

Beadeaux is a routing-first **partial** production map. Pages `1` and `15`
have page-owned native Detour graphs selected only by exact physical handoffs
and supported static destinations; production routing no longer requires the
dormant structure masks. Stock page `16` is a `Dummy Map` and remains
vanilla-only until an attended traversal proves whether the client assigns
reachable geometry to it.

The following completion evidence is still required:

1. verify the page-1 lower area and every route-affecting stair, ramp, door,
   mute device, afflictor, and projected crossing exposed by focused review;
2. verify every connector among the five retained page-15 native components;
3. capture entrance, midpoint, and exit X/Y/Z for each proven transition;
4. observe the active stock page throughout those routes, including every
   page-15 transition, to resolve page `16`;
5. classify the three nearby-component audit leads and any excluded component
   shown to affect a supported destination or normal route;
6. validate endpoint snapping, connected routes, deliberately disconnected
   targets, off-route recovery, and close-zoom path alignment in game.

Do not replace this section with a completeness claim until every item is
recorded below.

components containing an exact world handoff, the Survival Guide, or a
supported NM. It does not bridge terrain shelves, zone thresholds, or
overlapping elevations. No transition is inferred from proximity.

of route-relevant excluded native components, focused review of the isolated
Beaucedine boundary polygon, the missing Castle Zvahl outbound endpoint, four

# South Gustaberg (zone 107)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It replaces the unseeded 1,729-
component graph with native Detour topology selected by every exact world
handoff and the supported fixed NM positions. It requires no structure image
and authors no transition or blocked-link exception.

The production graph deliberately preserves three disconnected public
components:

- the 5,784-node main lowland contains the Bastok Mines, Metalworks, and
  eastern North Gustaberg handoffs plus Bubbly Bernie, Tococo, and one Leaping
  Lizzy position;
- the 2,019-node northwestern upland contains the Dangruf Wadi and western
  North Gustaberg handoffs plus the other Leaping Lizzy position; and
- the 373-node Carnero plateau contains both fixed Carnero positions.

The main lowland and northwestern upland have one nearby-component review
lead. No edge is authored without attended entrance/midpoint/exit evidence.
The Carnero plateau has no deterministic nearby-component lead within the
12-yalm audit radius. Destinations on another component remain marker-only.

## Stock calibration

The imported stock catalog identifies one page, `m_107_00`. Runtime stock
calibration should replace the legacy 768-pixel prototype transform and remain
authoritative through `stock_calibration = true`.

The installed `FFXiMain.dll` did not expose the expected static table
signature and AshitaMCP was unavailable during this audit, so the exact scale
byte and signed record offsets remain unresolved. The retained
`assets/maps/107.png` was not changed or recertified. This calibration gap and
the unavailable close/overview live checks block a completed-map claim.

## Native-component review

The unseeded native graph has 14,725 nodes, 14,427 edges, and 1,729
components. The production selector retains 8,176 nodes, 9,374 edges, and
three components:

| Nodes | Evidence and disposition |
|---:|---|
| 5,784 | Main lowland; Bastok Mines, Metalworks, eastern North Gustaberg, Bubbly Bernie, Tococo, and eastern Leaping Lizzy position |
| 2,019 | Northwestern upland; Dangruf Wadi, western North Gustaberg, and western Leaping Lizzy position |
| 373 | Carnero plateau; both fixed Carnero positions |

The other 1,726 components contain no exact handoff or supported fixed
destination. Most are isolated collision fragments, decorative/inaccessible
surfaces, or unrelated geometry and are not promoted by two-dimensional
proximity.

The selected graph has no stacked-floor overlap and one substantial
nearby-component lead:

```text
main lowland:       (-301.875,-392.875,-24.000)
northwestern upland:(-306.500,-394.375,-26.400)
planar separation: 4.862 yalms
elevation delta:   2.400 yalms
```

The elevation values above are Detour elevation, not live player Z. Proximity
does not prove a ramp, cliff path, or other player connection.

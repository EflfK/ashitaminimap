# South Gustaberg (zone 107)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It replaces the unseeded 1,729-
component graph with native Detour topology selected by every exact world
handoff and the supported ordinary NM positions. It requires no structure image
and authors no transition or blocked-link exception.

The production graph deliberately preserves three disconnected public
components:

- the 5,784-node main lowland contains the Bastok Mines, Metalworks, and
  eastern North Gustaberg handoffs plus Bubbly Bernie, Tococo, and the lowland
  Leaping Lizzy possibilities;
- the 2,019-node northwestern upland contains the Dangruf Wadi and western
  North Gustaberg handoffs plus the upland Leaping Lizzy possibilities;
- the 373-node Carnero plateau contains 48 scripted Carnero possibilities; and
- a newly retained 103-node lower Carnero plateau contains the remaining two
  scripted possibilities.

The main lowland and northwestern upland have one nearby-component review
lead. No edge is authored without attended entrance/midpoint/exit evidence.
The two Carnero plateaus have a nearby-component review lead, but their 11.334-
yalm elevation separation is not evidence of a player connection. Destinations
on another component remain marker-only.

## Stock calibration

The imported stock catalog identifies one page, `m_107_00`. Runtime stock
calibration should replace the legacy 768-pixel prototype transform and remain
authoritative through `stock_calibration = true`.

The installed `FFXiMain.dll` did not expose the expected static table
signature and AshitaMCP was unavailable during this audit, so the exact scale
byte and signed record offsets remain unresolved. The retained
`assets/maps/107.png` was not changed or recertified. This calibration gap and
the unavailable close/overview live checks block a completed-map claim.

## Notorious-monster source audit

Pinned active groups, full mob scripts, and the implemented question mark
support four ordinary NMs across 125 unique exact possibilities:

| NM | Level | Spawn type | Exact starts | PHs |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Bubbly Bernie | 9-10 | Triggered | 1 | 0 |
| Tococo | 4-5 | Timed/random start | 10 unique | 0 |
| Carnero | 11-12 | Lottery/random start | 50 | 9 |
| Leaping Lizzy | 10-11 | Lottery/random start | 64 | 2 |

Bubbly Bernie is popped at the implemented `qm1` near `(744,-671)` by trading
Quus, so its actor point is labeled Triggered rather than Fixed. Tococo's
script repeats the same ten positions five times; the catalog de-duplicates
those identical tuples while preserving every possible start and records the
scripted 3,600-4,200 second respawn. Carnero's 50 starts are split between 48
near live Z `-40` and two near `-60`, with its nine placeholder mappings split
7 and 2 by elevation. Leaping Lizzy's two actors and placeholders are split
into 33 lowland and 31 northwestern-upland starts.

Bhishani is Voidwatch, Bounding Belinda is Unity Wanted, Grylio is a dormant
zero-position record, and Pyracmon/Wraith Bats are event controlled. Their
encounter, zero, or event positions are excluded, as are ordinary mobs,
fished groups, helpers, and herd/event actors.

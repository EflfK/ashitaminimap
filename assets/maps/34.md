# Grand Palace of Hu'Xzoi (zone 34)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It retains ten substantial native
components selected by exact zone thresholds, gates, escort controls, map
Quasilumin, travel, and supported NM evidence. It does not invent links across
particle gates, Cermet Portals, escort barriers, trigger-area warps, elevators,
or event transports.

The stock catalog has pages `1`, `15`, and `16`, with page `1` as default. The
graph is deliberately zone-owned (`page_id = nil`) because pinned records do
not assign the retained components to stock pages.

components, including 67 isolated nodes. Ten exact supported selectors, chosen

elevator or stair connection. The other 118 source components lack supported
exact selectors.

## Notorious-monster source audit

The active static layer is derived from the pinned CatsEyeXI
`sql/mob_groups.sql`, `sql/mob_spawn_points.sql`, zone mob scripts,
`scripts/zones/Tahrongi_Canyon/Zone.lua`, and
`scripts/zones/Tahrongi_Canyon/npcs/qm2.lua`. CatsEyeXI SQL and Lua position
tuples are `(x, vertical, z)`; the third coordinate is live world Y and the
second is live player Z.

The four supportable references are:

| Name | Level | Type | Placeholders | Exact spawn possibilities `(x, y, live z)` |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Habrok | 16-18 | Timed/weather | 0 | `(-292.000,-540.000,15.000)` |
| Serpopard Ishtar | 19-20 | Lottery | 2 | `(-0.048,266.381,21.041)` |
| Herbage Hunter | 29 | Lottery | 1 | `(-127.000,466.000,30.000)`, `(-134.000,441.000,24.000)`, `(-144.000,460.000,24.000)` |
| Yara Ma Yha Who | 15-20 | Triggered | 0 | `(271.000,-334.000,0.005)` |

Habrok is enabled only during dust storm, sand storm, wind, or gales and uses
the zone script's 20-to-120-minute randomized timer. Serpopard Ishtar's two
lottery records share the one exact spawn possibility in its script. The
actual `phList` keys identify the south Wild Dhalmel at
`(-9.176,-64.347,-8.191)` and the north Wild Dhalmel at
`(-23.900,282.280,27.960)`; the north source comment names the adjacent
`(22.360,281.584,23.757)` record, but the executable `NM ID - 4` key is
authoritative. Herbage Hunter's placeholder is the Canyon Crawler at
`(-119.301,448.636,24.087)`. Yara Ma Yha Who is spawned at its fixed SQL
position by trading distilled water to `qm2`; it is therefore a triggered
reference rather than a timed or lottery marker.

The catalog-level `z` for Herbage Hunter records the first scripted spawn's
live elevation; the exact per-possibility elevations above remain the
authoritative source data. All three possibilities are on the same outdoor
surface.

### Explicit exclusions

- Yilbegan, Dawon, Tammuz, Chesma, Void Hare, and Prickly Sheep use the
  random-position Voidwalker system. Their `(1,1,1)` SQL rows are sentinels,
  not truthful static spawn points.
- Goblin Archaeologist has a zero-position disabled record and no Tahrongi
  activation script or deterministic spawn possibility in the pinned source.
- Smierc has two zero-position rows and one nonzero row, but its group is
  script-spawned and no pinned script enables any of the three Tahrongi
  records. The nonzero row alone is not evidence of an active static spawn.
- Wayward Worm and the five armor records have pool ID `0` and zero positions.
  Serpopard Ninlil and Otherworldly Rimester likewise have only zero-position
  Tahrongi rows and no zone activation path. These dormant/event records are
  not promoted to the map.
- Random Treasure Caskets, ordinary mobs, pets, and quest/event actors are not
  notorious-monster static references.

# Ghelsba Outpost (zone 140)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes native Detour topology
for every exact physical handoff, the active Survival Guide, and pinned NM
possibilities without requiring or generating a structure image. No transition or
blocked-link exception is authored.

The source navmesh is fragmented across six destination-bearing components:
the southern West Ronfaure/Yughott network, Fort Ghelsba threshold, Survival
Guide platform, northern Thousandarm area, Warchief/Fogweaver area, and the
three low-level fixed-NM camp. No selected component pair produces a
catalog-style transition lead within 12 yalms. Cross-component destinations
remain marker-only rather than being joined speculatively.

The stock catalog contains pages `1` and `15`, with page `1` the default.
Exact numeric map-table records and deterministic page/floor ownership were
not captured in this session. The graph is page-independent and runtime stock
calibration remains authoritative through `stock_calibration = true`; exact
page filtering remains unresolved.

every handoff, the Survival Guide, the five fixed NMs, and all 50 Thousandarm
possibilities. No mask, page, elevation band, transition, one-way transition,
or blocked-link argument is used.

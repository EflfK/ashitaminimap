# Ghelsba Outpost (zone 140)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes native Detour topology
for every exact physical handoff, the active Survival Guide, and pinned NM
possibilities with their complete active mechanics without requiring or
generating a structure image. No transition or blocked-link exception is
authored.

The source navmesh is fragmented across six destination-bearing components:
the southern West Ronfaure/Yughott network, Fort Ghelsba threshold, Survival
Guide platform, northern Thousandarm area, Warchief/Fogweaver area, and the
three low-level mission-NM camp. No selected component pair produces a
catalog-style transition lead within 12 yalms. Cross-component destinations
remain marker-only rather than being joined speculatively.

The stock catalog contains pages `1` and `15`, with page `1` the default.
The imported stock textures prove that both pages exist, but textures alone do
not encode the runtime position selector. Exact numeric map-table records and
deterministic page ownership were not captured in this session. Every supported
NM is outdoors and truthfully uses `floor = 'SURFACE'`; `page_id` is omitted so
the runtime-selected stock page remains authoritative rather than hiding a
marker behind a guessed page assignment.

# The Garden of Ru'Hmet (zone 35)

## Status and sources

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes 46 independently proved
native Detour components selected by exact boundary, arrival, Home Point,
recorded same-zone transfer, named-encounter, placeholder, and ordinary-mob
evidence. Twelve directed same-zone transfers from the connection catalog join
three of those source components, leaving 44 production routing components.

Cermet Portals, Ebon Panels, mission apparatus, doors, drops, or nearby
stacked-floor seams.

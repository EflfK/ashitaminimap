# Castle Zvahl Baileys (zone 161)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. The graph retains native Detour
components containing an exact world handoff, the Survival Guide, an active
treasure position, or a fixed supported named encounter. It does not bridge
doors, porter rooms, drops, thresholds, or overlapping floors. No transition
is inferred from proximity.

Completion requires live membership and automatic-switch confirmation for
stock pages `1`, `2`, `15`, and `16`; attended captures for doors and the four server-side
porter trigger areas; classification of excluded components and the two
destination-backed isolated nodes; review of eighteen native long edges,
38 separated-floor overlap pairs, and fifteen nearby-component leads; and an
addon reload with route/recovery testing.

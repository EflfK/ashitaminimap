# Yuhtunga Jungle (zone 123)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes native Detour topology
for every exact physical handoff, the Survival Guide, and the complete set of
ordinary static NM possibilities implemented by the pinned CatsEyeXI source.
No transition or blocked-link exception is authored.

The source mesh divides the supported destinations among seven native
components. Only the Norg, Survival Guide, central Yhoator thresholds,
Koropokkur, and two Pyuu possibilities share the broad main network. The Meww
possibilities and another Pyuu point share the Sea Serpent component; the
remaining Pyuu point, Rose Garden/Voluptuous Vilma,
Bayawak/Nasus, Mischievous Micholas, the northern and southern Yhoator
thresholds, and Ifrit's Cauldron remain distributed among separate native
components pending attended evidence. No cliff, tunnel, root bridge, ledge,
or zone seam is inferred from proximity.

The exact page-0 map-table record was not captured in this session. Runtime
stock calibration remains authoritative through `stock_calibration = true`,
but numeric scale and offsets must be recorded before the map can be described
as fully calibrated.

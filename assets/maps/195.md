# The Eldieme Necropolis (zone 195)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. The graph publishes only native
Detour topology for components containing a verified Batallia Downs handoff or
a supported CatsEyeXI destination. It deliberately does not bridge Eldieme's
many gates, floor drops, or navmesh seams. No structure image is required or
authored.

The remaining publication blockers are:

- live traversal confirmation of automatic switching across stock pages `15`,
  `16`, and `17`, beyond the current central-complex control;
- attended entrance/midpoint/exit captures for every route-affecting gate,
  drop, stair, and broken navmesh seam;
- classification of the 275 unselected native components, especially those
  near a selected component; and
- trustworthy spawn ranges and page/component ownership for quest- and
  mission-spawned encounters; and
- a live addon reload plus route, recovery, and page-filter verification.

Until those checks are complete, destinations on another selected component
remain marker-only. No proximity-based or visually inferred transition is
published.

## DLL-free automatic page selection

The 22 retained graph components are assigned to their stock artwork using the
exact page transforms above and one deterministic graph-node seed per
component: 13 components use page `15`, seven use page `16`, and two use page
`17`. Runtime `page_graph_seeds` snaps the live player to the retained graph
and selects the page assigned to that connected component, so page `14` is no
longer the automatic result on a covered component when `Minimap.dll` is
absent.

The live control at `(298.062, 19.981, -0.250)` in the central gate complex
snaps to the page-15 component near `(297.250, 19.625, -0.132)`. Components
excluded from this partial graph remain outside the automatic claim and fall
through to the normal stock/default behavior.

## Selected native components

The unseeded native graph has 4,774 nodes, 4,898 edges, and 297 components.
The production selector retains 2,394 nodes, 2,605 edges, and the following 22
components:

```text

```

The retained components account for all eight exact Batallia handoffs, the
Survival Guide, Hume Bones, the requested Titan's Gate reference, all ten
verified Treasure Chest positions, and all nine verified Treasure Coffer
positions. They also contain Cwn Cyrff's authored lottery spawn point on the
page-16 component of size 385; the lottery has four placeholders. The
four-node components are the isolated chest rooms around the central gate
mechanism; they are intentionally not joined to adjacent halls.

world-handoff seed and remain excluded pending focused classification.

No seed was guessed from a map-grid label. No `--transition`,
`--blocked-link`, elevation-band selector, or structure mask is used.

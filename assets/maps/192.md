## Page 2 D-10 correction overlay

The stock Beetle's Burrow artwork ends at the D-10 room even though its
walkable component continues south. Live traversal on CatsEyeXI confirmed the
player at `(-223.271, -218.457, 0.000)` after passing the Cracked Wall at
`(-220.013, -184.249, -2.072)`. Exact page-2 stock calibration places both
points immediately below the drawn room, proving an artwork omission rather
than a scale or origin error.

`192_02_structure.png` supplies a focused walkable overlay for this component.
It is generated from the pinned collision OBJ and native Detour topology with
the verified Treasure Chest seed `(-177.956, -220.058)`. The overlay retains
46 of 2,218 polygons; the remaining 159 native components stay unresolved and
the zone remains partial. Its component inventory is recorded in
`component-reports/192_02_structure.json`.

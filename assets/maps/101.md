# East Ronfaure (zone 101)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes native Detour topology
for every exact physical world handoff and the two pinned low-level fixed NMs
without requiring or generating a structure image. No transition or
blocked-link exception is authored.

The main outdoor surface is connected, but the eastern King Ranperre's Tomb
threshold and the Ranguemont Pass threshold remain separate source components.
The eastern Tomb component has one nearby-component review lead; proximity is
not evidence of a player connection. Both thresholds remain marker-only from
the main surface pending focused attended entrance, midpoint, and exit
captures.

The fresh endpoint audit also finds that five exact handoff records do not
snap within the runtime's 24-yalm radius at their recorded live Z: both
Southern San d'Oria south points, the Ghelsba outbound point, the western Tomb
outbound point, and the Southern San d'Oria west outbound point. This is
navmesh/endpoint evidence to review live, not permission to increase the
zone-wide snap radius or manufacture approach geometry.

The exact page-0 map-table record was not captured in this session. Runtime
stock calibration remains authoritative through `stock_calibration = true`,
but the numeric scale and offsets must be recorded before the map can be
described as fully calibrated.

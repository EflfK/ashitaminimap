# Yughott Grotto (zone 142)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes native Detour topology
for every exact physical handoff, all seven active Treasure Chest
possibilities, and Ashmaker Gotblut without requiring or generating a
structure image. No transition or blocked-link exception is authored.

The source is fragmented across 13 destination-bearing components. No selected
component pair produces a catalog-style transition lead within 12 yalms.
Cross-component destinations remain marker-only rather than being joined
speculatively.

The stock catalog contains pages `0`, `1`, and `2`, with page `0` the default.
Exact numeric map-table records and deterministic page/floor ownership were
not captured in this session. The graph is page-independent and runtime stock
calibration remains authoritative through `stock_calibration = true`; exact
page filtering remains unresolved.

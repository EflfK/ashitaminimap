# Valley of Sorrows (zone 128)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes deterministic native
Detour coverage for every source-backed public area found by the two Cape
Teriggan handoffs, the Survival Guide, the era-HNM basin, and one ordinary
mob point proving the eastern public arm. It does not require or generate a
structure image, and it authors no transition or blocked-link exception.

The stock catalog contains one page, page `0`. Runtime stock calibration is
authoritative through `stock_calibration = true`; the exact numeric map-table
record was not captured during this unattended pass.

# Batallia Downs (zone 105)

## Status

This is a routing-first **partial** map. It publishes native Detour topology
for every exact world handoff, the Survival Guide, the main outdoor surface,
the Eldieme-accessed plateaus, and the southern fixed-NM island. It requires no
structure image and authors no transition or blocked-link exception.

The graph intentionally preserves six disconnected source components. Three
seams still block a claim of completion:

- the three-node Survival Guide platform at `(-67,449,-2)` does not join the
  main outdoor component;
- the 13-node far-southern Eldieme zoning threshold does not join the
  144-node island containing Sturmtiger, Ahtu, and Suparna; and
- the 11-node Beaucedine zoning threshold does not join either the 1,638-node
  plateau or the main outdoor component.

Those gaps need focused attended entrance/midpoint/exit captures before any
`--transition` can be authored. The two nearby-component review leads also
remain unjoined. Destinations on another component remain marker-only.

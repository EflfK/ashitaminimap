# Level 1-60 production-map queue

This queue covers combat and travel zones that become materially relevant by
level 60. It excludes cities, residential areas, ships, battlefields,
Dynamis/Abyssea variants, and zones whose ordinary use begins after level 60.
The tiers are an authoring priority, not a claim that every monster in a zone
falls inside the listed range.

Work proceeds in batches of three zones. A checked map satisfies the
routing-first completion invariant. `Partial` means the repository contains
useful authored work but still has a documented routing, page, destination, or
live-verification gap. `Re-audit` means a graph predates the present completion
gate.

partials and re-audits stay in their natural level tier; they are not treated
as complete merely because a graph or authored asset exists.
